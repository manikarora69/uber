## Verdict

I can understand the product, its MVP boundary, the rider capabilities, cross-functional dependencies, and what blocks a launch decision from the PRD alone.

## Ranked gaps before using it to commit build or launch

1. **Market and service configuration:** OQ-1–OQ-4 leave city/zone, categories, payment methods, and fare/cancellation policy open. These determine what the first release can actually offer.
2. **Safety and local operating readiness:** OQ-5–OQ-6 and OQ-10 need the selected verification, emergency/support capabilities, and staffed escalation model for each market.
3. **Measurement and reliability gates:** OQ-8 and OQ-11 need baselines, target thresholds, stopping rules, and service budgets before pilot expansion.
4. **User evidence:** UJ-1 is an abstract flow from the source, not a named observed session. OQ-12 should be resolved before UX sign-off.

These gaps are explicitly captured as open questions; they do not prevent the PRD from serving as the starting point for product research and epic refinement.
