## Verdict

This draft gives teams a coherent initiative-level frame for a new Uber-like rider app and can guide epic refinement. It is not ready to commit a market launch because the launch city, ride categories, payment and fee policies, numeric targets, service budgets, and local data/safety obligations remain unresolved.

## Three fixes that matter most

1. Confirm the launch market, zones, ride categories, and payment methods before finalizing MVP and matching scope (Open Questions OQ-1–OQ-3).
2. Set policy, safety, support, and local data requirements before pilot approval (OQ-4–OQ-6, OQ-9–OQ-10).
3. Agree baselines, guardrails, and measurable performance/availability budgets before pilot (OQ-8 and OQ-11).

## Template done-when

- Requirements trace to the source-provided booking journey or rider needs; detailed flows remain an open research item.
- Scope and deferrals are explicit.
- Success measures name counter-metrics or guardrails; numeric baselines/targets remain open.
- The level and length fit an initiative PRD.
- The draft makes no unsupported market, regulatory, vendor, or metric claims.
