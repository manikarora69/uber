- (requirement) R1 (prd FR-1): Rider can register, authenticate, recover access, and maintain a valid session with market-supported verification.
- (requirement) R2 (prd FR-2): Rider can review completed and cancelled trips with route summary, status, fare, and receipt when applicable.
- (requirement) R3 (prd FR-3): Rider can set and adjust pickup and destination using location, search, or map selection, with manual pickup when location permission is denied.
- (requirement) R4 (prd FR-4): Product validates serviceability and route feasibility before request submission, explains unsupported locations, and offers recovery.
- (requirement) R5 (prd FR-5): Rider can compare eligible ride categories by fare estimate/range, pickup ETA, and relevant attributes before confirming.
- (requirement) R6 (prd FR-6): Product discloses fare components, fees, and dynamic pricing before confirmation and captures rider confirmation for high-demand pricing.
- (requirement) R7 (prd FR-7): Rider can submit a uniquely identified request once; repeated submissions do not create duplicate active requests.
- (requirement) R8 (prd FR-8): Product reports matching progress, match/no-driver outcome, driver cancellation, and recovery without stale request state.
- (requirement) R9 (prd FR-9): After match, rider can view available driver/vehicle identifiers, current pickup ETA, and approach state.
- (requirement) R10 (prd FR-10): Rider can coordinate pickup and reach the driver through supported in-app communication, masking personal contact details where supported.
- (requirement) R11 (prd FR-11): Rider can cancel and understand any applicable fee before confirming cancellation where feasible.
- (requirement) R12 (prd FR-12): During a trip, rider can view status, destination, route, and estimated arrival, with last-update time when data is stale.
- (requirement) R13 (prd FR-13): Rider can share approved trip information with a trusted contact and access emergency assistance and support during a trip.
- (requirement) R14 (prd FR-14): Product gives rider a prominent way to report driver/vehicle mismatch or another urgent safety concern before or during a trip.
- (requirement) R15 (prd FR-15): After completion, rider can see final fare and an itemized receipt consistent with the payment record.
- (requirement) R16 (prd FR-16): Product reports payment success/failure and supports safe retry or eligible alternative without duplicate successful charges.
- (requirement) R17 (prd FR-17): Rider can rate a completed trip and report safety, conduct, lost-item, fare, payment, or trip issues in context.
- (requirement) R18 (prd FR-18): Rider can track support responses/case status and find localized self-service help with escalation.
- (constraint) C1 (prd RULE-1): A request requires an authenticated rider, valid pickup/destination, eligible category, and market-approved payment arrangement.
- (constraint) C2 (prd RULE-2): Only eligible, available, policy-compliant drivers may receive requests; a driver holds at most one active trip, subject to policy.
- (constraint) C3 (prd RULE-3): Request creation and payment processing are idempotent; retries do not duplicate active bookings or successful charges.
- (constraint) C4 (prd RULE-4): A trip starts only after driver start and any configured pickup verification.
- (constraint) C5 (prd RULE-5): Estimates are not final fares unless fixed-price; dynamic pricing and fees are disclosed under approved policy and local requirements.
- (constraint) C6 (prd RULE-6): Emergency assistance must not imply guaranteed dispatch unless a supported local integration provides it.
- (constraint) C7 (prd RULE-7): On location/network/notification failure, preserve request/trip state, show staleness, and provide recovery.
- (constraint) C8 (prd RULE-8): Core controls support screen readers, text scaling, sufficient contrast and touch targets; color alone never conveys state.
- (constraint) C9 (prd RULE-9): Minimize and protect personal/location data, restrict access, approve retention, and keep direct identifiers out of general analytics.
- (constraint) C10 (prd RULE-10): Configure language, currency, date/time, address formats, ride attributes, and disclosures by enabled market.
- (constraint) C11 (prd RULE-11): Before public pilot, critical journeys have logs, metrics, traces, alerts, and operational dashboards.
